Jira issue lists Mercury shows you now come from a deterministic formatter instead of being freely composed in prose each time — same wording and layout every time, with a clickable link per issue. Set `JIRA_SITE_URL` to your Jira site (e.g. `https://webcomperio.atlassian.net`) to enable it; without it, results are returned exactly as before.
